Interpretation For Claude Code

The run validates the launcher/gate path and ADR-011 probe scheduling mechanically: the gate saw the expected forced probes at ep80 and ep280, and the full run completed without invalid or loss-cap collapse. The reward split/probe changes also surfaced high-value fusion evidence: the highest raw reward is a forced block2 fusion probe at episode 80.

The remaining issue is policy adoption. The long-run tail is dominated by no-fusion actions and final PPO entropy is effectively zero. The diagnostics rank-best candidate is fusion_count=0 even though forced block2 fusion reached a slightly higher raw reward. Next work should focus on why PPO/rank selection does not retain the profitable block2/block5 fusion evidence, not on map validity or replan installation.
